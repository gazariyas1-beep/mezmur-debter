Privacy Policy

Mezmur Debter Tewahedo app · Last updated: November 4, 2026

Mezmur Debter Tewahedo shares Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox Tewahedo hymns with their videos and lyrics. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no ads.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

To work the way you expect, the app keeps a few things in your device's own storage. They never leave your device, and we cannot see them:

  • Hymns you save with the heart button, and your recently played list
  • Your choices, such as light or dark mode, lyrics text size, and the daily reminder time
  • A saved copy of the app, the hymn list, and the lyrics, so they open quickly and work without internet

You can erase all of this by clearing the app's data in your browser or phone settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Services that load content

The app uses a few outside services to show videos, pictures, and fonts. When your device asks them for content, they can see standard technical information such as your IP address and device type, under their own privacy policies:

  • YouTube (Google) plays the hymn videos through its embedded player and provides the video cover pictures. Using it is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts provides the text styles used in the app.
  • Our hosting provider delivers the app's files and keeps ordinary server logs, as most websites do.

Notifications

In the store versions of the app, you can turn on a daily hymn reminder. These reminders are created on your device. No information about you is sent to us to make them work. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

Children

The app does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app works in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
